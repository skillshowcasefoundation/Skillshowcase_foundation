SKILL SHOWCASE FOUNDATION WEBSITE — QUICK START

FILES
- index.html: website content
- styles.css: responsive design
- script.js: small interaction/year update
- assets/logo.png: provided Foundation emblem
- assets/official-rules.pdf: provided rules PDF

IMPORTANT BEFORE PUBLISHING
1. Add your live Google Form URL to the Register button in index.html. Search for id="register" and replace href="#contact" with your Google Form URL. Update its visible text from “Registration link to be added” to “Register Now”.
2. Verify the competition date, exam time window, age categories and final prize details against your current Google Form and approved Terms & Conditions. The source rules document and recent planning details may not say the same thing in every place.
3. Add the actual Instagram profile URL to the Instagram contact card if desired.
4. Publish only once all details are confirmed.

FREE PUBLISHING WITH GITHUB PAGES
A. Sign in/create an account at https://github.com/
B. Create a new public repository named skill-showcase-foundation
C. Upload index.html, styles.css, script.js, README.txt and the assets folder with its files. Upload files/folders, not the ZIP file itself.
D. In the repository, open Settings → Pages.
E. Under Build and deployment, choose “Deploy from a branch”, select “main” and “/(root)”, then Save.
F. Wait for GitHub Pages to publish. Your free site URL will look like https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/skill-showcase-foundation/ .

ALTERNATIVE: Google Sites
This package is a custom-coded website and cannot be imported as a full design into Google Sites. To use Google Sites, recreate the pages there and link the rules PDF and Google Form.

PRIVACY / SAFETY
Do not put participant personal data, private registration responses, or private payment details into public website files. Link to a Google Form configured to collect only the information you need.
